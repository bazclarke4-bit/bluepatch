Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

The short version: we don't collect any personal information. There are no accounts, no adverts, no tracking and no cookies. Anything you type, like a nickname, stays on your own device.

Who we are

Adventures of Blu and Pals is made by Blue Patch Studio, based in the UK. You can contact us at hello@bluepatchstudio.com.

What is saved, and where

To remember your settings, the game saves a few things in your web browser's own storage on your device (called "local storage"):

  • the nicknames you type in (optional) and which pal each player picked
  • whether you chose 1 or 2 players
  • your sound setting and your best scores

This information never leaves your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we can't see it. To delete it, clear this website's data in your browser settings.

Cookies, tracking and adverts

We don't use cookies, analytics, tracking or adverts. If we ever add adverts or a visitor counter, we will update this page first and explain what changes.

Our web host

The website is hosted by IONOS. Like all web hosts, their servers may keep short technical logs (such as IP addresses and the time of a visit) to keep the site secure and running. We don't use these logs to identify anyone.

Children

The game is made for families and is suitable for children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Grown-ups: please encourage children to use a fun nickname rather than their real full name.

Emails

If you email us, we will only use your email to reply to you, and we won't share it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
